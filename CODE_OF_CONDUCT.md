# Code of Conduct

AgentConnector welcomes contributors of every background and experience level. Everyone participating in this project deserves respectful treatment, including people with disabilities and people whose identity, beliefs, or circumstances differ from their own.

## Community expectations

- Discuss ideas and code with care. Explain disagreements with evidence and suggest practical improvements.
- Welcome questions, help newcomers, and give people time to learn.
- Respect names, pronouns, accessibility needs, and communication preferences.
- Accept feedback and take responsibility for the effect of your words and actions.
- Protect other people's privacy and obtain consent before sharing their personal information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual advances or sexualized content, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, and publishing private information without permission are unacceptable. Repeated disruption, unwanted contact after a request to stop, and retaliation against someone who reports a concern are also unacceptable.

## Scope and responsibility

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces. It also applies when someone represents AgentConnector in a public setting. Maintainers are responsible for applying these standards fairly, including to themselves.

## Reporting concerns

For content on GitHub, open its menu and choose **Report content**. If **Report to repository admins** is available, use it to notify the maintainers privately. Otherwise, use **Report abuse to GitHub Support**, or [GitHub's abuse reporting form](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse). See [GitHub's reporting instructions](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) for available options. Reports sent to GitHub Support are handled by GitHub; they are not a direct message to this project's maintainers.

Include links to relevant content and enough context to explain the concern. Do not create a public issue containing private incident details, personal information, or credentials. Security vulnerabilities should follow the separate [security policy](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers who receive a report will review it fairly and limit sharing to what is necessary to investigate and act. A maintainer involved in a complaint should not decide its outcome. You may report concerns about a maintainer directly to GitHub Support using the same reporting options.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and request a change in behavior. Depending on severity and repetition, they may issue a warning, restrict participation temporarily, or ban a participant. Serious threats or harassment may warrant an immediate ban. Maintainers should explain moderation decisions to the affected person when doing so is safe and respect the privacy of those involved.
